Herd Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Herd lets members of a private group see which of their group's usual spots people are at right now. This page explains what the app collects and why.

Your location stays on your phone

Herd uses iOS region monitoring to notice when you arrive at or leave one of your group's spots. That check happens on your device. Your GPS coordinates are never sent to our servers or to anyone else. When you arrive somewhere, Herd asks you to confirm; only the spot you confirm (for example "WU") is shared.

Optional auto check-in. If you turn on Auto check-in in Settings (it is off by default), Herd checks you in without asking after you have been at a spot for about 2 minutes, and notifies you so you can undo it. To do this, your phone tells our server when it enters or leaves one of the group's spots; your GPS coordinates are still never sent. You can turn it off at any time.

What we store

  • The name you enter when you join a group.
  • Your current spot and when you got there, after you confirm it (or automatically, if you turned on Auto check-in). It is deleted when you leave or tap "I left". Check-ins at dining spots are also deleted automatically after 8 hours, and at the gym after 3 hours.
  • An anonymous account ID created by the app so it can recognize you. No email, phone number, or password is collected.
  • A notification token for your device, if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you when a spot gets busy.

Who can see it

Only people who joined your group with its join code can see your name and current spot. We do not sell, rent, or share your information, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States, and is protected by access rules that limit it to your group's members.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. To remove your name and account from our servers, tap Leave group in the app's Settings, which deletes them right away. You can also ask your group's organizer or open a request at github.com/benyahuta/herd-privacy/issues, and we will delete it.

Children

Herd is intended for college students and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
